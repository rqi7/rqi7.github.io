Research profile

Detecting threats in smart grids.

My work develops data-driven security methods for advanced metering infrastructure and power-system data.

My research develops semi-supervised and unsupervised anomaly-detection methods for smart-grid security. It combines deep representation learning, feature engineering, and ensemble learning to detect electricity theft and cyber-attacks in AMI and PMU data.
